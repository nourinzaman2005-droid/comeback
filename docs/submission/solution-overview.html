Solution overview | 1 of 5

ComeBack

A local-first, clinician-connected companion that turns the ICC Return to Play Post-Pregnancy Guidelines into a clear, measurable journey back to cricket.

For the player

One next step in her language, on her phone.

For the clinician

Movement observations, symptoms, and approval control.

For the ICC

A practical digital layer for a new Member-board framework.

01

Problem | 2 of 5

A guideline cannot help if the daily journey is unclear.

The current gap

  • The ICC framework is published as a nine-page PDF.
  • Return support must be individualised across physical, psychological, social, and practical needs.
  • Players and care teams need a shared view of actions, symptoms, and decisions.
“The guidelines serve as a template for Members, and strong support needs to be individualised to the specific needs of them and their family.”
Dr Philippa Inge, ICC Medical Advisory Committee

Source: ICC launch release and ICC Return to Play Post Pregnancy Guidelines, pp. 2-5, 22 Jun 2026.

Feasibility | 4 of 5

A working prototype, not a concept screen.

Frontend

  • Installable GitHub Pages PWA
  • Offline IndexedDB journey
  • English, Bengali, Hindi, Urdu
  • Accessible mobile-first UI

Intelligence

  • Four pose-supported tests
  • Deterministic 6 Rs rules
  • Cited no-key explanation fallback
  • Optional free Groq wording layer

Care connection

  • Clinician queue and review
  • Server-side symptom block
  • Render/PostgreSQL Blueprint
  • Signed red-flag webhook option
Verified locally: 59 unit/API tests, 12 mobile and desktop journeys, 100% rules coverage, and all files below 40 MB.
04

Impact | 5 of 5

A pilot path for more supported returns.

Immediate value

  • Players see a clear, translated next step.
  • Clinicians retain approval authority.
  • Member boards gain an auditable implementation layer.
  • Offline and phone-first design broadens access.

Responsible pilot

  1. Clinical and legal review.
  2. One Member-board usability pilot.
  3. Measure completion, clarity, accessibility, and clinician response.
  4. Adapt for other sports only with sport-specific evidence.
ComeBack does not replace a care team. It helps the player and care team move through the same plan together.
