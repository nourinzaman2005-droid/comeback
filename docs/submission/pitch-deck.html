ComeBack

The clinician-connected, camera-assisted journey that turns the ICC Return to Play Post-Pregnancy Guidelines into practical support on any phone.

Built by Nourin Zaman and Ashra Rahman Tuba 01

The human problem

Motherhood should not end a sporting career.

“No player should have to choose between motherhood and representing her country.”
Jay Shah, ICC Chairman

A new framework, but still a document

The ICC published a nine-page Return to Play Post-Pregnancy framework in June 2026. Players and care teams still need a practical way to follow it day by day.

The gap is not another workout app. It is an understandable, measurable, clinician-led return journey.

Sources: ICC, “ICC launches Return to Play Post-Pregnancy Guidelines,” 22 Jun 2026; ICC Return to Play Post Pregnancy Guidelines, pp. 2-7.

02

Our answer

One calm path from recovery to cricket.

1

Personal roadmap

The ICC 6 Rs become clear next steps with source references.

2

Private camera checks

On-device pose estimates reps, time, asymmetry, depth, and FPPA indicators.

3

Symptom safety gate

Reported symptoms stop approval and direct the player to clinical review.

4

Clinician decision

A clinician reviews metrics and approves or holds the next stage.

AI explains. Camera observes. Only the clinician approves.

Sources: ICC Guidelines, pp. 3 and 6-7; Goom, Donnelly and Brockwell, Returning to Running Postnatal, 2019, pp. 13-18.

Clinical sync

  • Render Express API
  • PostgreSQL metrics store
  • Signed role sessions
  • Server-enforced symptom block
59 unit and API tests, 12 mobile and desktop journeys, 100% deterministic-rules coverage, and zero npm audit findings.
05

Safety by architecture

We do not automate medical clearance.

Hard boundaries

  • Clinician approval is mandatory
  • Symptoms block advancement
  • AI cannot alter the stored stage
  • Camera metrics are observations, not diagnoses
  • No camera-media column exists

Evidence boundaries

The ICC document supplies the 6 Rs framework and care-team guidance. Movement screens come from separate Level 4 expert consensus and are presented for clinician review, never as ICC pass thresholds.

Sources: ICC Guidelines, pp. 2-7; Goom et al. 2019, pp. 3, 13, 17; Donnelly et al., “Reframing return-to-sport postpartum: the 6 Rs framework,” BJSM, 2022.

07

ICC pilot plan

Start small, measure safely, then expand.

1

Clinical review

Have an ICC-aligned sports physician and women’s-health physiotherapist review wording, rules, and escalation.

2

Member pilot

Run an 8 to 12 week usability pilot with one Member board, its care team, and a small voluntary cohort.

3

Measure

Track completion, clinician response time, reported clarity, accessibility, and opt-in retention. Do not claim medical efficacy from a prototype pilot.

Pilot success means safer coordination and clearer support, not an automated return-to-play decision.
08

Sustainable scale

Built for cricket, adaptable across sport.

ICC value

A configurable Member-board platform can digitise a common framework while allowing local care pathways, languages, legislation, and clinical ownership.

Cross-sport path

The care-team workflow, symptom gates, on-device observations, and clinician review can support other sports. Each sport must supply and validate its own content and rules.

A hosted institutional plan can fund maintenance while the player-facing experience remains affordable and accessible.
